\begin{description}
\item[(a)] From the graph, the magnitudes at minimum light of the pulsation
  cycle before and during Great Dimming are
  \[ m_1 = 0.9, \qquad m_2 = 1.65 \]
  The difference in magnitude between the two minimum light phases is
  \[ \Delta m = 0.75 \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{ccc}
  \toprule
  Half mark & Full mark & Half mark \\
  \midrule
  $0.65 \le \Delta m < 0.70$ & $0.70 \le \Delta m \le 0.80$ &
  $0.80 < \Delta m \le 0.85$ \\
  \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  Call $F_0$ the initial brightness of the star. The fractional decrease in
  the observed brightness caused by the dust clump is
  \[ k_1 = \frac{F}{F_0} = 10^{-\frac{\Delta m}{2.5}} \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  Therefore,
  \[ k_1 = 0.50 \]
  \hfill(0.5 points)

\item[(b)] Call $F_{\mathrm{clump}}$ the brightness transmitted through the
  dust clump. The observed brightness of the star during the dimming is
  \[ F = 0.20F_0 + 0.80F_{\mathrm{clump}} \]
  \hfill(1.5 points)

  Hence,
  \[ k_2 = \frac{F_{\mathrm{clump}}}{F_0}
         = \frac{\dfrac{F}{F_0} - 0.20}{0.80} = 0.38 \]
  \hfill(1 point)

\item[(c)] The brightness transmitted through the dust clump is
  \[ F_{\mathrm{clump}} = F_0 e^{-\tau} \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  Hence, the optical depth is
  \[ \tau = -\ln\left(\frac{F_{\mathrm{clump}}}{F_0}\right) = 0.97 \]
  \hfill(0.5 points)

\item[(d)] Call $\rho$ the density of the dust clump, $\Delta z$ the average
  extent of the clump along the line of sight.

  The optical depth is
  \[ \tau = \rho\kappa\Delta z \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  The dust clump has a volume
  \[ V = 0.80\pi R^2 \Delta z \]
  \hfill(0.5 points)

  and a mass
  \[ M_{\mathrm{dust}} = \rho V \]

  Therefore,
  \[ M_{\mathrm{dust}} = 0.80\pi R^2 \frac{\tau}{\kappa} \]
  \hfill(1 point)
  \[ M_{\mathrm{dust}} = 5.9 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{M}_\odot \]
  \hfill(0.5 points)

  The total mass of the ejected material is
  \[ M_{\mathrm{eject}} = 201 M_{\mathrm{dust}}
     = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{M}_\odot \]
  \hfill(0.5 points)
\end{description}
