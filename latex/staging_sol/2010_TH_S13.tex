The Wien law is
\begin{equation*}
\lambda_{\max} = \frac{0.29}{T}\,(\mathrm{cm}) \tag*{5}
\end{equation*}
So the temperature is
\begin{equation*}
T = \frac{0.29}{550\times 10^{-9}} = 5272 \approx 5300~\mathrm{K} \tag*{5}
\end{equation*}
Or
\[
T = \frac{0.29}{500\times 10^{-9}} = 5800~\mathrm{K}
\]
Note: $5200\sim6000$\,K all full mark.
