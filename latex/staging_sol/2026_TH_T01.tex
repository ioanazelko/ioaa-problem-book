\begin{description}
\item[(a)] Based on the law of conservation of energy, we have
  \[ \frac{mV_\infty^2}{2} = \frac{mv_p^2}{2} - \frac{GmM_\odot}{r_p}
     \quad (1) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  where $m$ is the mass of the ISO, $r_p$ is the perihelion distance, and
  $v_p$ is the perihelion velocity. Besides, using the law of conservation of
  angular momentum gives us
  \[ mpV_\infty = mr_p v_p \quad (2) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  Combining Eq. (1) and Eq. (2) leads to
  \[ \frac{p^2 V_\infty^2}{r_p^2} - \frac{2GM_\odot}{r_p} - V_\infty^2 = 0
     \quad (3) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  Solving Eq. (3) with $r_p > 0$ provides
  \[ r_p = \frac{p^2 V_\infty^2}
                {GM_\odot + \sqrt{G^2 M_\odot^2 + p^2 V_\infty^4}}
     \quad (4) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  Our numerical result is
  \[ r_p = 5.05 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{m} \quad (5) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

\item[(b)] Let $R_{\mathrm{ISO}}$ be the radius of the ISO. Assuming an ideal
  blackbody and fast rotation, we have
  \[ \left(\frac{L_\odot}{4\pi r_p^2}\right)\pi R_{\mathrm{ISO}}^2
     = 4\pi R_{\mathrm{ISO}}^2 \sigma T^4 \quad (6) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  Thus, the equilibrium surface temperature of the ISO is
  \[ T = \left(\frac{L_\odot}{16\pi\sigma r_p^2}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}
       = 4.8 \times 10^2\,\mathrm{K} \quad (7) \]
  \hfill(0.5 points)

  This value is well above the sublimation temperature of water ice in
  vacuum, so sublimation is expected if exposed water ice is present.
  \hfill(0.5 points)

\item[(c)] The magnitude difference $\Delta m$ is related to the flux ratio
  $F_{\mathrm{max}}/F_{\mathrm{min}}$ by
  \[ \Delta m = 2.5\log_{10}\left(\frac{F_{\mathrm{max}}}{F_{\mathrm{min}}}\right)
     \quad (8) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  Since the brightness change is entirely due to projected area, we obtain
  \[ \frac{F_{\mathrm{max}}}{F_{\mathrm{min}}} = \frac{a}{b} \quad (9) \]
  \hfill(1 point)

  where $a/b$ is the major-to-minor axis ratio. Combining Eqs. (8) and (9)
  yields
  \[ \frac{a}{b} = 10^{\Delta m/2.5} = 6.3 \quad (10) \]
  \hfill(1 point)
\end{description}
