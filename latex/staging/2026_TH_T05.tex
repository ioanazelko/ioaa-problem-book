A primordial black hole (PBH) is a hypothetical black hole, which is expected
to be formed very soon after the Big Bang.

\begin{description}
\item[(a)] (5 points) Find the expression of the initial mass $M$ of a PBH,
  provided that the cosmological density at a time $t$ after the Big Bang is
  $\rho_c \sim 1/(Gt^2)$ and the PBH is assumed to be a homogeneous sphere
  having a radius identical to the Schwarzschild radius.
\item[(b)] (5 points) Estimate the initial mass and formation time of PBHs
  after the Big Bang such that they are still evaporating, provided that the
  black hole evaporation time is
  $\tau(M) = 10^{67}\left(M/M_\odot\right)^3$ (yr).
\end{description}
