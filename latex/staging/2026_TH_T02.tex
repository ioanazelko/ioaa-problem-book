A binary system, whose masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ are hierarchical,
$m_1 \gg m_2$, and separated by a distance $R$, circularly orbits its center
of mass, $C$. The rotating reference frame $Cxyz$ has the $x$-axis pointing
from $m_1$ to $m_2$ and the $z$-axis oriented with the angular velocity of
the system, $\vec{\omega}$. A satellite whose mass $m_3$ is much smaller than
$m_1$, $m_2$ oscillates in the $z$-direction about the Lagrange point $L_1$
lying on the line segment that connects $m_1$ and $m_2$. Let $\delta_1$ and
$\delta_2$ be the distances from $C$ to $m_1$ and $L_1$ to $m_2$,
respectively.

  $z$-axis.

  satellite in the $z$-direction for the Earth-Sun system.
\end{description}
